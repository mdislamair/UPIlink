Payment blocked or declined?

UPI apps sometimes stop payments that start from a link or a QR code, often as a security check. Paying from inside your app avoids the link:

  1. Open your UPI app and choose Pay to UPI ID.
  2. Paste the UPI ID below.
  3. Enter the amount and pay.
